The service

Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

Fibres hold on to dirt, allergens and odours. Our hot-water extraction process lifts them out, brightening colours and extending the life of your carpets, rugs and upholstery.

What's included

  • Pre-inspection and spot treatment of stains
  • Deep hot-water extraction for carpets and rugs
  • Sofas, armchairs and fabric dining chairs
  • Mattress refresh on request
  • Odour removal and freshening
  • Fast drying times
  • Safe for families and pets

Ideal for

Homes with children or pets, rental properties and offices with high foot traffic.

Available in London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Liverpool, Sheffield, Bristol and Newcastle.
