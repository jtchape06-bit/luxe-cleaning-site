Our story

Cleaning, elevated

Luxe Cleaning Co was created with a simple belief: a clean space should feel like a luxury. We combine professional methods with an eye for detail and a warm, dependable service to deliver results our clients are proud of.

Whether it's a family home, a city apartment, a rental property or a busy office, we tailor every clean to the space and the person behind it. Our team arrives fully equipped, works carefully and leaves things immaculate.

  • Homes and businesses in the UK's major cities
  • Regular, one-off and bespoke cleaning
  • Quality products and professional equipment
